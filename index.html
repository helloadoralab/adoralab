Adora Lab Legal & Policies

Transparent Creative Workflows • Zero Geographical Limits

Data Privacy & Brand Confidentiality

Adora Lab collects only essential contact details (name, email, WhatsApp/message) and raw product photos strictly for project communication and creative production. All raw product photos, unreleased designs, and brand assets remain 100% confidential and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Service Deliverables & Revisions

All commercial videos, UGC ads, and brand posters are delivered digitally in Full HD (1080p) within an average of 3–4 days (zero physical shipping). Packages include 2 free revisions for Starter/UGC tiers and up to 4 free revisions for Booster/Scale tiers.

50/50 Payment Structure & Refund Policy

Projects operate on a transparent 50% advance deposit to initiate production and 50% balance upon final approval/delivery, processed securely via Wise or Direct Bank Transfer. Because custom AI creative production requires immediate studio compute and art direction, the 50% advance deposit is non-refundable once production begins; however, we guarantee revision support within your package scope to ensure complete brand alignment.

Intellectual Property & Portfolio Rights

Upon full (100%) payment, the client receives full commercial usage rights for all final delivered video and poster assets. Adora Lab retains the right to showcase completed deliverables in its agency portfolio and social channels unless a prior Non-Disclosure Request (NDA) is agreed upon in writing.

Official Contact

For any privacy, NDA, data deletion, or support inquiries, contact hello.adoralabs@gmail.com.
